I've become obsessed with FPV drones.
This page records flights, so I can keep track of whether I'm getting better or worse, and make memories.
I fly an **[Air75 II](https://betafpv.com/products/air75-ii-brushless-whoop-quadcopter)** Whoop (**Syl <i class='fa-solid fa-drone-syl' aria-hidden='true'></i>**) and a larger custom Seeker 3 (**Kaladin <i class='fa-solid fa-drone-kaladin' aria-hidden='true'></i>**) with a **[Radiomaster Pocket](https://radiomasterrc.com/products/pocket-crush-radio-controller?variant=46486325067968)** controller using a **[HDZero BoxPro+](https://www.hd-zero.com/product-page/hdzero-boxpro-boxpro)**.

I'm also maintaining a community server for [**Propwash FPV**](https://propwashfpv.com/), a Minecraft FPV mod that, while not as accurate as [The Zone](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3491280/The_Zone__FPV_Drone_Simulator/) (which I still play from time to time) is significantly more fun since you can just... build and fly.
Come join @ `mc.propwashfpv.com`!
