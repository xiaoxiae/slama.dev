**Everything you read is written by me.**
I do not, nor will I ever, use AI to write the articles that you read, the thoughts that I try to convey or the images and illustrations that I create.
I do employ LLMs to look for grammatical, stylistic, and factual errors in the articles that I write, and if they find valid issues, **I** make the changes.

That being said, I **heavily use LLMs for programming + implementation** of this website -- creating automated scripts that cut up the videos you see on the [drone](/drone) page, the service that runs [Printomat](/printomat), and other such things.

LLMs are a useful tool and can be used for good and evil.
I'm still finding the right balance, and will update this page should my views on AI usage on this website and in general change.
